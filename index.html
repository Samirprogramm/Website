Questions before checkout.

Short answers for use boundaries, firmware, shipping, returns, and what is included.

What is Ducky V1 for?
Ducky V1 is built for education, diagnostics, and authorized wireless research in environments you own or have explicit permission to test.
Is it legal to use?
Legality depends on where and how it is used. Only use the device within applicable laws and on systems, signals, and devices you are allowed to inspect.
What is in the box?
The Ducky V1 package includes the main unit, USB-C cable, antenna set, quick-start card, and a safety note. Optional cases and enclosures can be added separately.
Can I change the firmware?
Ducky V1 is designed as a controlled learning device. Firmware access and extension options are provided with the product documentation.
Where do you ship?
The shop is optimized for Switzerland. International shipping can be requested through support before payment.
